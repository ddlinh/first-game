🎮 Game Review & Feature Proposal Document

Focus: Roguelite Core Mechanics, Polish, & Ecosystem Evolution System

1. Current Build Issues & Feedback (Bug List)

  • 1. Missing Onboarding: Lacks tutorial for basic controls, narrative intro, and clear run objectives.
  • 2. Camera Jitter/Stutter: Unstable camera movement ruins the combat smoothness in roguelite runs.
  • 3. Missing Directional Sprites: Character only has a front-facing sprite; missing side and back movement animations.
  • 4. Character Scale Issue: Main character sprite is oversized relative to the screen viewport.
  • 5. Collision & Clipping: No physical barriers; the character clips under and passes through environmental structures.
  • 6. Combat UI & Visual Polish: Battle interface is cluttered and lacks aesthetic quality compared to references like Hades or Cult of the Lamb.
  • 7. Lack of Level-up Feedback: Stat upgrades and power progression upon leveling up are unclear and lack impact.
  • 8. Rescued NPC Behavior: NPCs vanish immediately upon rescue instead of accompanying the player through stages before returning home.

2. Roguelite Competitor Analysis & Benchmarking

Compared to market leaders (Hades, Cult of the Lamb), the project currently requires improvement in 4 core pillars:

  1. Meta-Progression & Clarity: Short-term run perks and long-term base upgrades need stronger visual and numerical feedback.
  2. Game Feel & Combat "Juice": Tight collision, accurate sprite directions, and smooth camera work are baseline requirements for fast-paced combat.
  3. World Immersion: NPCs trailing the player or reacting to the environment significantly heightens emotional investment.
  4. UI Visual Hierarchy: Health bars, cooldowns, and status effects must be readable at a glance without obstructing view.

3. Feature Proposal: Ecosystem Resonance System

A novel progression and weapon evolution mechanics based on natural biology, food webs, and environmental elements.

A. Biological Kingdoms (Race/Tộc)

  • Flora Plant Kingdom: Focuses on regeneration, root control, and bio-toxins. Passive: Photosynthesis (Heals in sunlight/standing still).
  • Fauna Animal Kingdom: Focuses on attack speed, lifesteal, and critical rage. Passive: Predatory Instinct (Increased crit as target HP drops).
  • Mineral Earth Kingdom: Focuses on damage reduction, poise, and shockwaves. Passive: Mass Density (Converts physical damage taken into area shockwaves).
  • Mycelium Fungi/Microbe Kingdom: Focuses on infection, decay, and minion spawn. Passive: Spore Network (Infections chain-spread between nearby enemies).

B. Elemental Energies (System/Hệ)

  • Thermal (Fire/Magma): Damage over time, explosions, armor melt.
  • Aquatic (Water/Frost): Slows, freezes, corrosion, fluid area control.
  • Atmospheric (Wind/Lightning): Chain damage, high mobility, speed boosts.
  • Organic (Poison/Decay): Stat debuffs, lifesteal, environmental corruption.

C. Weapon Evolution Mechanic: "Food Chain Devour"

Weapons do not use gold/gem upgrades. Instead, weapons devour biological monster cores to mutate along evolution trees.

[Base Weapon] + [Devoured Core Type] = [Mutated Form]

Example Evolution Path (Base Iron Sword):

  1. Feeds on Flora Core + Aquatic: Mutates into Vine Whip-Blade (Attacks drag enemies in and entangle them).
  2. Feeds further on Fauna Core + Organic: Mutates into Hydra's Brier (Thorn blade that steals enemy HP on hit).
  3. Feeds further on Mineral Core + Thermal: Reaches Final Form Obsidian Core Breaker (Volcanic glass blade creating magma fissures).
