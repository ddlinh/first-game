Master System Design & Concept Document

REKINDLED

A Post-Apocalyptic 1 to 4 Player Roguelite Farming & Civilization-Rebuilding Game

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

REKINDLED is a 1 to 4 Player (Solo & Co-op) Roguelite featuring Farming & Settlement Meta-progression set in a post-apocalyptic world where human civilization has collapsed into ash. Unlike traditional roguelites focused solely on looting weapons or inanimate materials, the most valuable resources in Rekindled are People and the Vocational Knowledge they preserve.

Core Philosophy: Civilization resides not in stones or objects, but in the people who hold the crafts. Every survivor rescued from the underground ruins is an ember blown back to reignite human society.

Player Scale Flexibility (1 to 4 Players)

The game seamlessly supports both Solo Play and Multiplayer Co-op (up to 4 players). Dynamic scaling adjusts enemy health, spawn density, and resource drop rates according to party size. In solo play, players can recruit AI Mercenary Companions from the village to assist in dungeon runs.

Dual-Layer Gameplay Loop

  • Expedition Layer (Roguelite Run): Procedurally generated dungeon crawls into ancient subterranean ruins. Permadeath applies to personal run loot. Main Goal: Rescue trapped survivors, collect "Knowledge Relics" (blueprints, seed specimens, machinery parts), and collect temporary combat buffs.
  • Settlement Layer (Persistent Village Meta): The shared surface village. Never reset upon death. Houses farming, crafting, research, and village growth. As the population grows, the village automates production and supplies future expeditions.

2. VISUAL DIRECTION & 2.5D DYNAMIC CAMERA SYSTEM

The visual identity of Rekindled bridges cute pixel charm with 2.5D depth contrast, utilizing high-detail pixel art layered over a 3D environment with dynamic dynamic lighting and smooth zoom capabilities.

1. Art Style & Perspective Specifications

  • Perspective2.5D Isometric / Orthographic Depth: 2D pixel character billboards rendered inside a 2.5D environment with 3D terrain depth and dynamic shadow casting (similar to Cult of the Lamb, Eiyuden Chronicle, and modern retro RPGs).
  • AestheticsCute Pixel Art / Modern Retro: High-resolution pixel art featuring charming, expressive character proportions inspired by Pokémon top-down readability and Cult of the Lamb's character appeal.
  • ProportionsChibi Pixel Silhouettes: Big-head, compact-body ratios (1:3 scale). Ensures clear character readability across all zoom levels.
  • Palette ContrastDual Tone Palette:
    • Subterranean Ruins: Cold, desaturated pixel palette (ash grey, steel purple, murky indigo). Hostile and dead.
    • Surface Village: Rich, warm palette (ember orange #FF6B35, golden amber, sprout green, soil brown). Warm and vibrant.
  • VFXPixel "Rising Embers": Signature pixel particle effect. Stylized ember sparks rise upward during successful rescues, research breakthroughs, and village upgrades.

2. Dynamic Zoom Mechanics (Zoom In / Zoom Out)

The camera features a seamless multi-tier dynamic zoom system (via mouse wheel or controller triggers) to serve both micro-detail immersion and macro-tactical management:

3. IN-RUN BUFF SYSTEM & ESCORT MECHANICS

To enhance combat depth during runs, players can acquire temporary run-buffs. In the demo, NPC rescues are smooth and streamlined without heavy movement debuffs.

1. In-Run Temporary Buffs (Run Power-Ups)

During subterranean expeditions, players discover Ember Shrines, Run Relics, and Combat Drops that provide temporary stackable buffs for the current run:

  • Weapon Quality Buffs: Temporarily increases weapon tier, unlocks elemental affinity (Fire, Frost, Electric), or adds attack range/cleave.
  • Speed & Agility Buffs: Increases movement speed, dash cooldown speed, and attack speed.
  • Armor & Toughness Buffs: Grants temporary shield layers, flat damage reduction, or knockback immunity.

2. Demo NPC Escort Rules (No Debuffs)

Streamlined Demo Rescue: In the initial Demo build, rescued NPCs do NOT inflict movement speed penalties or weapon restrictions on players. Rescuing an NPC immediately activates their active support buff while they follow the party safely to the village portal.

3. Full Game "Injured Status" (Post-Demo Mechanics)

In the full release, certain NPCs may carry an "Injured Debuff" requiring carrying/escort tradeoffs, granting unique active professional perks while carried.

4. WORKER ALLOCATION & CONSTRUCTION PROGRESSION FLOW

To preserve logical depth and role specialization, construction and production operate on a clear division of labor between Domain Specialists and Builder Engineers.

1. Role Separation Architecture

  • Domain Specialists (Farmers, Smiths, Apothecaries, Scholars): Propose new infrastructure blueprints, establish ingredient requests, and operate completed facilities. They do not physically erect buildings.
  • Builder / Construction Engineers (Industry Pillar): The dedicated physical construction workforce. They carry materials, construct scaffolding, and erect infrastructure.

2. Dynamic Construction Flow (Pre-Rescue to Automation)

To ensure game progression never halts early on, construction moves through three distinct phases:

PHASE 1: MANUAL SCAFFOLDING
Before rescuing a Builder NPC, placing a blueprint creates Pixel Scaffolding. Auto-builds at 5% speed, or players manually hammer (E Key) to finish.
➔
PHASE 2: BUILDER NPC AUTOMATION
Rescuing a Builder NPC fully automates building! The Builder fetches wood/stone, hammers with dust/spark VFX, building 5x-10x faster.
➔
PHASE 3: INDUSTRIAL CRANE TIER
Upgrading the Builder Workshop to Tier 3 unlocks Steam Cranes & Assembly Crews for instant/near-instant city construction.
Early-Game Onboarding Design: Forcing players to manually hammer their first 1-2 buildings creates a satisfying emotional connection ("laying the first stones of civilization by hand"). Rescuing the first Builder NPC then triggers a huge Automation Reward Moment as the Builder takes over all construction labor.

1. Norfolk Four-Course Crop Rotation Epoch 1 — Demo Available
Prerequisite: None (Starter Agriculture Quest)

Historical Context: The 18th-century British Agricultural Revolution eliminated the traditional fallow year by rotating Wheat, Turnips, Barley, and Clover—naturally replenishing nitrogen without depleting the soil.

Ingredient Request: 3x Ancient Wheat Seeds + 3x Turnip Seeds + 3x Nitrogen Clover Seeds.

Gameplay Reward: Unlocks the "Self-Restoring Quadrant Grid". Planting these 4 crops in adjacent 2x2 plots prevents soil degradation forever and grants a passive +50% yield bonus.

2. Hydraulic Bloomery & Water-Bellows Epoch 1 — Demo Available
Prerequisite: Complete Quest 1 (Farmer provides food stamina for forge labor)

Historical Context: Harnessing river waterwheels to drive air bellows increased forge temperatures beyond 1200°C, transforming brittle wrought iron into durable high-carbon steel.

Ingredient Request: 5x Crude Iron Ore + 2x Giant Wooden Gears + 1x Hydraulic Piston Joint.

Gameplay Reward: Upgrades the Hand Forge into the Hydraulic Forge (Tier 2). Automatically pumps air via the river, unlocking the crafting recipe for Indigo Steel Weapons.

3. Gutenberg Movable Type Printing Press (1440) Epoch 2 — Full Game
Prerequisite: Complete Quest 2 (Requires Hydraulic Forge for metal type casting)

Historical Context: Johannes Gutenberg invented movable lead type printing in 1440, enabling mass production of books and accelerating the European Renaissance.

Ingredient Request: 5x Lead/Tin Ingot Bars + 3x Waterproof Oil Ink Jars + 10x Wood-Pulp Paper.

Gameplay Reward: Constructs the Village Printing Press. Permanently reduces all research completion times and village tier evolution times by 50% across the settlement.

4. Newcomen Atmospheric Steam Engine (1712) Epoch 2 — Full Game
Prerequisite: Complete Quest 2 & 3 (Requires Copper Boiler + Printed Schematics)

Historical Context: Thomas Newcomen built the first practical steam engine in 1712 specifically to pump floodwaters out of deep mine shafts, allowing miners to reach deeper ore veins.

Ingredient Request: 1x Copper Boiler Tank + 4x Pressure Pipes + 10x Chunk Coal.

Gameplay Reward: Activates the Newcomen Drainage Pump at the ruin entrance. Drains flooded dungeon rooms on Floor 3, uncovering secret vault doors and cutting dungeon navigation time by 40%.

5. Penicillin Mold Cultivation (1928) Epoch 3 — Full Game
Prerequisite: Complete Quest 4 (Requires Steam Boiler for lab distillation)

Historical Context: Alexander Fleming discovered Penicillium mold in 1928, creating the world's first true antibiotic and revolutionizing medicine.

Ingredient Request: 2x Blue Bread Mold Samples + 3x Bioluminescent Fungi + 1x Steam Distiller.

Gameplay Reward: Upgrades the Herbalist to an Apothecary (Tier 2). Unlocks the craftable Antibiotic Serum, granting 15 minutes of complete immunity to toxic/poison debuffs during runs.

8. TECHNICAL FEEDBACK & LIGHTING SHADERS

Global Warmth Index (GWI Shader System)

A global shader value `GWI` (`0.0` to `1.0`) dynamically alters the game world in real-time:

  • GWI = 0.0 (Game Start): Cold grey desaturated tones, thick fog, pitch-black village.
  • GWI = 0.5 (Mid-Game): Warm amber light beams from workshop windows. Forge glows red.
  • GWI = 1.0 (End-Game): Full Ember Orange radiance. Floating **Rising Embers** particle system active.

9. FIRST DEMO SCOPE SPECIFICATIONS

  • Player Scale: 1 to 4 Players (Solo with optional AI Companion, or 2-4 Player Co-op).
  • Environment: 1 Subterranean Ruin biome (3 Floors).
  • Camera System: 2.5D Isometric perspective with dynamic Zoom In (conversations/animations) & Zoom Out (8x8 village grid/boss fights).
  • Escort Rule: NO NPC Injured Debuffs in Demo. Rescuing NPCs provides immediate active buffs without movement penalty.
  • In-Run Buffs: Stackable temporary buffs for Weapon Quality/DMG, Movement Speed, and Armor/Shields.
  • Construction Mechanics: Features Manual Scaffolding Hammering early on, transitioning into Builder NPC automation upon rescue.
  • Constructible Infrastructure: Includes Tier 1 & Tier 2 buildings for Agriculture (Raised Bed, Windmill) and Metallurgy (Clay Forge, Hydraulic Forge).
  • Demo Quest Scope: Quest 1 (Norfolk Rotation) & Quest 2 (Hydraulic Forge) available. Quests 3-5 previewed in Tech Tree.
  • Post-Rescue Feedback: Rescued NPCs at the village request specific ingredients (e.g., seeds/metals) to unlock new crops and forged gear.
  • NPC Pool: 5 Villagers representing the 5 Pillars (1 per pillar).
  • Evolution Preview: Test Tier 1 → Tier 2 evolution for Farmer & Blacksmith.
  • Features: 8x8 Grid village building, 1 Cross-Research combo (*Indigo Steel*), GWI Lighting Shader, and Rising Embers VFX.
